### [Warcraft III - Reign Of Chaos](https://uk.webuy.com/product-detail?id=3348542135680) You'll need your CD-ROM

1. Read the CD-ROM into .iso

2. Install patch [1.27b](https://us.battle.net/download/getLegacy?product=WAR3&locale=enUS&os=WIN)

- Container Variant: **bionic**
- Wine Version: **proton-9.0-x86_64**
- Executable Path: **Warcraft III/Warcraft III.exe**
- Exec Arguments: **-opengl**
- Screen Size: **854x480**
- Audio Driver: **PulseAudio**
- Graphic Driver: **Wrapper**
- Graphic Driver Version: **turnip26.0.0_R8**
- DX Wrapper: **2.7.1**
- Max Device Memory: **0 MB**
- Box64 Version: **0.4.0**
- Box64 Preset: **Performance**
- Direct3D: **Native (Windows)**
- DirectSound: **Bultin (Wine)**
- DirectMusic: **Bultin (Wine)**
- DirectShow: **Native (Windows)** <-- Cinematics will not render without this
- DirectPlay: **Native (Windows)**
